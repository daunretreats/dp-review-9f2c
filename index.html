Implant Centre

A complete implant facility.

Dentalpro's Implant Centre is a complete facility which offers the professional care of a highly qualified and experienced team of specialists. A dental implant is ideal for replacement of teeth lost due to disease, accidents, or even where teeth are congenitally missing.

  • Natural appearance and function, and the most stable and durable option compared with other methods of tooth replacement
  • Promotes bone growth, and in some cases helps maintain the shape of the face
  • Planned and restored within the same centre, by the specialists who treat you

Dental financing

Syariah-compliant medical or dental financing, repaid by direct debit, with no collateral needed. Financing up to 100% of the procedure or treatment cost (maximum RM30,000), over a repayment period of up to 36 months. For KL and Selangor residents only.

Health tourism

Malaysia, for the treatment and the trip.

Malaysia has long been established as a trusted destination for healthcare travel: high quality services at competitive prices, in an environment conducive to recovery and recuperation. Because most dental procedures do not result in downtime, patients can enjoy the leisure activities of Kuala Lumpur in between treatments.

Dentalpro has over 20 years' experience as a provider for dental tourism, and understands the concerns that are unique to travelling patients: treatment accuracy, time frames, logistics, post-treatment care and personal assistance. Our Customer Service team takes you step by step from first contact through treatment, departure and beyond. Complimentary transport to and from the clinic can be arranged when you share your accommodation details.
